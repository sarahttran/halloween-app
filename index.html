Stay Spooky, Stay Safe!

Heading out to collect treats? Remember:

  • Under 18? Explore with a parent or trusted adult.
  • Stay on sidewalks and watch for cars.
  • Never enter a stranger's home or vehicle.
  • Don't share your live location or personal information with strangers.
  • Map pins are community-submitted and may not be verified. Check before visiting.
