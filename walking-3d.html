Welcome to Walking Mode

Follow your own animated character through a real neighborhood layout with pastel 3D buildings, winding roads and Halloween candy markers.

Real map: Streets and building footprints come from OpenStreetMap through Overpass. If loading fails, a clearly marked sample neighborhood is displayed.

Candy: Your group's Firestore houses load from firebase.js when available. Gold demo treats are fictional and can be switched off.

Only your own avatar appears. GPS starts only after you press ◎. This demo does not publish anyone's location.
